# Privacy Policy — Big Face (DRAFT)

> **Note:** This is a starter draft, not legal advice. Have a licensed attorney review before public launch.

**Last updated:** 2026-10-14

## 1. What we collect
- **Account info:** username, email, password hash, display name, gender (optional), bio, profile color, avatar choices.
- **Content:** messages, reactions, uploaded images/files, media posts, video/audio transmitted during calls (we do not record calls; WebRTC streams go peer-to-peer).
- **Activity:** XP, likes, gifts, token transactions, payout records, reports.
- **Technical:** IP address, device/browser info, basic logs for security.

## 2. What we DON'T collect
We follow data minimization: no ad tracking, no sale of personal data, no third-party analytics beacons in this build.

## 3. How we use it
To run the Service (chat, video, leaderboards), keep accounts secure, process payments via Stripe, prevent abuse, and comply with the law.

## 4. Sharing
- Other users see your public profile, messages in shared rooms, and media you post.
- **Stripe** processes payments and payouts (their privacy policy applies to payment data).
- We share data with authorities when legally required, and to protect safety.

## 5. Storage & security
Passwords are bcrypt-hashed. Traffic should always use HTTPS in production. Data is stored on our servers (PostgreSQL + file storage). No system is perfectly secure, but we use industry-standard practices.

## 6. Your rights
You can view, correct, or delete your account data at any time (settings → delete account). Transaction records needed for legal/financial compliance are retained. EU/UK users have GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection) — contact us to exercise them.

## 7. Children
The Service is not directed at children under 13. If we learn a child under 13 registered, we delete the account.

## 8. Changes
We'll notify users of material changes before they take effect.
